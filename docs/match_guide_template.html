The MATCH tab

One robot, one field, running live. This is where you drive a robot yourself, watch an AI strategy drive it instead, and put a whole roster of robots on the field at once to see what happens. Everything else in the app -- STRATEGY, SWEEP, SEARCH -- exists to feed a better strategy or a better design back into this tab.

This guide assumes you've never opened the simulator before. If you only read one section, read "The one rule that trips everyone up" -- it explains why nothing you type on this tab seems to do anything at first.

01Walkthrough: the tab, panel by panel

Three columns. The left column configures who's on the field and how they're built. The middle is the field itself, with a console log and the play/pause transport bar underneath. The right column is what's happening right now -- live numbers, match-wide settings, and a reminder of which keys drive the robot.

02The one rule that trips everyone up

Read this before you touch anything

Almost every control on the left and right columns -- chassis speed, manipulators, scoring timing, friendly collisions, the whole roster -- carries the same small print: "Changes apply on RESET." Typing a new max speed does nothing to the robot currently on the field. It only becomes true the next time you press RESET, which throws away the current match and starts a fresh one with whatever is configured right now.

This is deliberate, not a bug: a robot's top speed changing mid-flight isn't a real scenario worth simulating, and "everything you've set up takes effect together, at one well-defined moment" is easier to reason about than "some things are live and some aren't." But it means the most common way to think the app is broken is to change a setting, stare at the field, and wonder why nothing moved. Press RESET.

A gamepad is used automatically if one is plugged in when the app starts -- the CONTROLS panel on the right always shows whichever set is actually active. Holding ALGAE, the deposit target switches itself to PROCESSOR or NET the moment the robot is sitting in one of those zones; you don't select a level for algae.

If you'd rather watch than drive, check AI drives primary robot in the roster panel and pick a strategy -- your robot then drives itself, the same way every other AI robot on the field does.

04Walkthrough: putting more robots on the field

A real match has six robots, not one. Add AI-controlled teammates and opponents from the ROSTER panel, each running whichever strategy you pick for it -- and each getting its own tab under ROBOT CONFIG, so a fast robot and a slow one can share the field honestly.

05Playing, pausing, and reviewing a match

The bar under the field controls time. Press play and the match runs at normal speed -- fifteen seconds of AUTO, then teleop, same as a real one. Pause it and the scrub bar wakes up: drag it and every robot, piece, and score jumps back to exactly what it was at that point, the same way scrubbing a video does.

That's the whole trick worth knowing: scrubbing only works while paused. Dragging the bar mid-match doesn't mean anything, because the physics simulation doesn't have a "rewind" -- it only has "replay what was already recorded." Pause first, then scrub.

Run as fast as possible (roster panel, once AI drives primary is checked) advances the sim as fast as the CPU allows instead of at real-time speed -- useful for watching a whole match resolve in a few seconds rather than two and a half minutes.

07When something looks wrong

SymptomWhat is going on
I changed a setting and nothing happened See "The one rule that trips everyone up" above. Press RESET.
Dragging the scrub bar does nothing It's locked while the match is playing. Pause first (the play/pause button, or Start on a gamepad).
WASD doesn't move the robot The field doesn't have keyboard focus -- click on it first. Also check that AI drives primary robot isn't checked; a human and an AI can't both drive PRIMARY.
An AI teammate keeps stealing my game piece Not a bug -- two robots running strategies that both want the same piece will genuinely compete for it, exactly as two real robots would. Try Disable friendly collisions in MATCH SETTINGS if they're physically colliding rather than just racing.
The match ended and nothing is happening Expected -- once time runs out, MATCH stops advancing on purpose (it also stops logging telemetry, so it doesn't fill up with duplicate frames while the window sits open). Press RESET to start another.

08Glossary

PRIMARY
The one robot that exists no matter what -- the one WASD/gamepad drives by default, and the one every other tab (STRATEGY, SWEEP, SEARCH) treats as "your" robot.
Roster
Every robot beyond PRIMARY: an alliance (blue/red), a strategy, and its own chassis settings under ROBOT CONFIG.
AI intent
An overlay showing what an AI robot is currently aiming for and which tactic it's running -- the fastest way to tell whether a strategy is doing something sensible or something confused.
Telemetry
Live numbers for whichever robot is selected: position, speed, what it's holding, current score. Recorded every tick so the scrub bar can play it back.
