GAME 01 · 4-PIECE CAPACITY

Aurora Array

Alliances fill their private, three-tier ARRAY with AURORA CELLS. Three early ARRAY scores unlock access to a shared center CROWN, producing a choice between fast local cycles and the more valuable contested route.

Field layout

Nominal field: 690 x 317 in. Origin is the lower-left corner at the blue ALLIANCE WALL; red features are the 180-degree rotation of blue features. Two SOLAR RAILS create north, center, and south crossing lanes.

Scoring and match rules

Score a CELL when it is fully supported by an ARRAY shelf, or passes through a CROWN window and remains in its catch tray. The CROWN starts locked for each alliance and unlocks permanently when that alliance has scored three ARRAY CELLS. A CROWN attempt while locked scores zero.

Endgame

In the final 30 seconds, a fully supported, stationary ROBOT on its own AURORA PAD earns 8 points. Two or three parked ROBOTS earn another 6 points if their bumpers are within the pad boundary.

Bonus RP

  • Radiance: 30 ARRAY points and one score on every tier.
  • Crown: unlock CROWN and score 4 CROWN CELLS.
  • Alignment: at least 2 parked ROBOTS.

StrategySim brief: Use `aurora_cell`, capacity 4; ARRAY scoring regions with tier capacities; a passive, shared CROWN region; unlimited ports; and floor spawn regions. Add small game-specific checks for the three-score CROWN unlock and per-alliance CROWN cap. Compare 1-, 2-, and 4-CELL intake designs.

GAME 02 · 5-PIECE CAPACITY

Rivet Rush

Alliances build their bridge with a single flexible RIVET ring. Low-risk DECK slots score immediately, while an ARCH must be completed in order before it unlocks the contested central SPAN. This is a placement and sequencing game, not a shooting game.

Field layout

RIVET: 4 in foam ring. Holding limit: 5 RIVETs; preload: up to 3 per ROBOT. The GIRDER blocks the direct central route, while the two neutral RACKS reward decisive trips.

LandmarkBlue location / role
DECK`(155, 78)`; six one-RIVET floor sockets
ARCH`(230, 220)`; lower hooks must be scored before upper hooks
DEPOT`(85, 250)`; unlimited alliance supply
RACKS`(290, 105)` and `(400, 212)`; 12 neutral RIVETs each
SPAN`(345, 158)`; four shared hooks, initially unavailable

Scoring and match rules

A RIVET must remain seated in a socket or captured on a hook. Each ARCH's paired lower hook must be full before its upper hook scores. Completing both lower and both upper hooks completes that alliance's ARCH and unlocks its access to SPAN.

Endgame

A ROBOT raised above its 60 x 42 in alliance-wall LIFT DECK scores 6 points, or 10 if its alliance completed the ARCH. The deck supports two robots; a third sheltered beneath it earns 4 points.

Bonus RP

  • Structure: complete ARCH and score 3 DECK RIVETs.
  • Span: score 2 SPAN RIVETs.
  • Lift: score 20 ENDGAME points.

StrategySim brief: Use a single `rivet` with capacity 5. Make every socket/hook a capacity-one scoring region. Add a game-specific “blocked until scored” prerequisite for ARCH upper hooks and SPAN. Use unlimited DEPOTs, 36 finite loose RIVETs, and the GIRDER obstacle. Compare batch delivery against early SPAN denial.

GAME 03 · 2-PIECE CAPACITY

Vector Vault

Alliances choose reliable NEST placement, long-range VAULT shots, or a limited central CORE. It supports an effective low-goal partner while rewarding accuracy, then asks robots to choose between late cycles and a shared endgame traversal bar.

Field layout

VECTOR: 8 in foam disc. Holding limit: 2 VECTORs; preload: 1 per ROBOT. The VAULT and CORE are passive targets: a released shot scores without the ROBOT entering the target region.

LandmarkBlue location / role
NEST`(92, 158)`; 72 x 58 in low placement target
VAULT`(210, 158)`; 36 x 96 in passive high target
TRANSFER STATIONS`(38, 58)` and `(38, 259)`; unlimited supply
CORE`(345, 158)`; six shared high-value ports
TRAVERSAL BAR`(115, 158)`; 84 x 18 in climbing structure

Scoring and match rules

A VECTOR scores once. NEST discs are deposited in a floor bin; VAULT and CORE discs are launched and caught. CORE ports are covered during AUTO, then open at the beginning of TELEOP. Once an alliance scores its third TELEOP VAULT VECTOR, it receives one 20-second VECTOR BURST: its own VAULT shots score 7 rather than 5 during that window.

NEST32Unlimited private floor-bin deposits

Endgame

A latched ROBOT, fully supported by its TRAVERSAL BAR with wheels off carpet, earns 10 points. Two latched robots earn an extra 6-point synchronized traversal bonus. A robot fully under its bar earns 3 park points.

Bonus RP

  • Velocity: score 18 AUTO points.
  • Precision: score 5 VAULTs and 1 CORE.
  • Traversal: 2 latched, or 1 latched plus 2 parked.

StrategySim brief: Register `vector` with capacity 2. Use active NEST scoring plus passive VAULT/CORE regions, six capacity-one CORE ports, and an alliance-specific 20-second scoring timer for the burst. Model posts and CORE base as obstacles. Sweep shot reliability, shot time, and climbing time to find whether a specialist shooter or balanced climber wins.

Qualification and source notes

Across all three games, a qualification win earns 3 RP, a tie 1 RP, and a loss 0 RP; listed task RPs are qualification-only. The ideas use recurring shapes documented in FRC_GAME_TRENDS.md: a 15-second AUTO, 135-second TELEOP, a final-30-second endgame, higher AUTO values, protected areas, finite capacity where it creates strategy, and achievement-based RP. The sectioning follows the practical game-description pattern of the supplied 2024 manual.
